Story

Named after their own song

Twisted Dragon is a blues hard rock band from the Kansas City metro, trading in thunderous grooves, fuzz-soaked riffs and the raw swagger of '70s rock. For fans of Royal Blood, Queens of the Stone Age, Wolfmother and Crobot.

The band started around 2017 as Skewer. When a cease and desist forced a new name, they took it from one of their own songs, about a fictional nightclub called Twisted Dragon. Founders Garrett Kleitz and William "Lightning" Keen spent the next years writing and self-producing a four-part EP series, released together in 2025 as the album Long Live Rock and Roll.

After a three-year break, the band returned in October 2026 and is writing new material.
