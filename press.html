The story

A name taken from their own song

Twisted Dragon is a blues hard rock band from the Kansas City metro, trading in thunderous grooves, fuzz-soaked riffs and the raw swagger of '70s rock.

The band formed around 2017 as Skewer. After a cease and desist forced a name change, they took the name of one of their own songs, a track about a fictional nightclub called Twisted Dragon. Founders Garrett Kleitz and William "Lightning" Keen spent the next several years writing and self-producing a four-part EP series (Leave Your Mind, Free Your Body, Give Your Soul and Long Live Rock and Roll), released together as the album Long Live Rock and Roll in 2025, followed by the single "Delirium."

In 2023 the band expanded with Dawson Kleitz and Benjamin Blevins, then stepped back while members pursued other projects. In October 2026, Twisted Dragon returned as a three-piece with a high-energy comeback show at Westport Bowery, and is now writing new material.
